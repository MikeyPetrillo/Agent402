# Text-to-Speech

> **Payment wires:** every paid endpoint accepts **x402** and **MPP** (Machine Payments Protocol) on the same 402 - see [[Paying with x402]] and [[Paying with MPP]]. Agent402 is the applied layer of [[Agentic Finance]]: agents that pay and get paid on their own.

> **Acceptable use.** Hosted-instance traffic is governed by the [Terms of Service](https://agent402.tools/terms) - including a generative-content acceptable-use policy - and by the upstream model providers' usage policies. Wallets used for prohibited content are blocked before settlement. Outputs are generated by third-party models from your inputs; you are responsible for how you use them.


Three tiers of text-to-speech, paywalled via x402. Send text, get back base64-encoded audio. The same request shape and the same 10 voice names on every tier. Model-backed: third-party speech models generate the audio, and the answer's `model` field names the one that spoke.

## Tiers

| Endpoint | Price | Quality | Formats | Text cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/tts-lite` | $0.005 | Synthetic-sounding, the cheapest | mp3, pcm | 800 chars |
| `POST /api/tts` | from $0.005, by length | Natural, low latency | all six | 2,000 chars |
| `POST /api/tts-hd` | from $0.010, by length | Most expressive; reads audio tags such as `[whispering]` | all six | 2,000 chars |

**Pricing by length.** `tts` and `tts-hd` are quoted per request from the text: `tts` is $0.005 plus $0.0575 per 1,000 characters, `tts-hd` is $0.010 plus $0.115 per 1,000 characters, each rounded up to the next $0.001. 2,000 characters, the cap, costs $0.120 on `tts` and $0.240 on `tts-hd`. The 402 for a request carries its quote, and `/api/pricing` lists each tier's range and formula (`quoteRange`, `quoteFormula`). `tts-lite` is flat.

If a tier's first model is unavailable, the call is served by a backup speech model instead of failing; every backup serves all six formats. A call that timed out or came back empty is not retried, since it may already have been generated; it fails, and on x402 that failure is not charged. Some calls are paid before they run; a failed one can be retried free with the same payment, or is refunded. `/api/gateway-status` reports `speechUpstream` as `ok`, `cooling` or `probing`.

**Which to use.** The lite tier is for high-volume narration, notifications and agent speech, where the cost per call matters more than the timbre; its voice is audibly synthetic next to the other two tiers, which is the trade. Ask it for a format it cannot serve and you get a 400 pointing at `/api/tts`, never a silent downgrade.

All three tiers are **wallet-only**. See [[Security Model]].

## Voices

`alloy` (default), `ash`, `ballad`, `coral`, `echo`, `fable`, `nova`, `onyx`, `sage`, `shimmer`. Each tier maps a name to its nearest voice and names the voice that spoke in the answer. `GET /api/pricing` and each tier's page at `/tools/<slug>` list the other voice names a tier takes.

## Output formats

`mp3` (default), `opus`, `aac`, `flac`, `wav`, `pcm`

## Request / Response

```json
// Request
{ "text": "Hello from Agent402!", "voice": "alloy", "format": "mp3" }

// Response
{ "model": "<the model that spoke>", "voice": "river", "format": "mp3",
  "audio": "<base64-encoded audio>", "chars": 20 }
```

Only `text` is required. `voice` defaults to `alloy`, `format` defaults to `mp3`.
