# Code of Conduct

Agent402.Tools is maintained by Havok Holdings LLC. Everyone who takes part in
this project - issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews - is expected to
keep it a place where people can do good work.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Keep security reports private until a fix ships (see SECURITY.md).
- Respect that maintainers decide scope; a declined change is not a judgment
  of the person who proposed it.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of reviews or discussions.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to mike@agent402.tools. Reports are read by the
maintainer, handled privately, and acted on: a warning, a temporary block, or
a permanent block from the project, depending on the case. Retaliation against
a reporter is itself a violation.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, kept
short on purpose.
